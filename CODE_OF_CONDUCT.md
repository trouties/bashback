# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant][homepage], version 2.1, as its
Code of Conduct. The full text is published at:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: we pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, and to act in ways that foster an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported privately to the maintainer
([@trouties](https://github.com/trouties)). All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly.

[homepage]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org
